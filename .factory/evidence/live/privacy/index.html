Your recordings

Privacy, in plain language

Effective August 28, 2026. Line Take Match keeps unreleased dialogue on your device.

Audio and take data

Audio analysis runs in your browser. Recordings, measurements, flags, line names, and notes stay in browser storage. They are not uploaded to us. Removing site data removes your take list unless you exported a Studio backup.

Demo data

The demo uses a separate browser database named demo:line-take-match. It never reads or writes your real take list. “Start for real” clears the demo database.

Payments and licenses

Sociobot hosts Studio checkout, with Dodo as merchant of record. This app sends only your license token to Sociobot for verification. Payment details never reach this app. Your license and its daily verification result stay in browser storage.

Network and analytics

The app sends no analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, transcription, or voice-generation requests. Your hosting provider may keep short-lived access logs for security.

Your choices

Export a CSV at any time. Studio users can download a backup with audio. You can remove takes, site data, or your local license.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site operator through Sociobot (external site).
