Privacy, in plain language

Effective August 28, 2026. Line Take Match is designed so unreleased dialogue stays with you.

Audio and project data

Audio analysis runs in your browser. Recordings, measurements, flags, line names, and notes are stored in IndexedDB on your device. They are not uploaded to us. Removing browser site data removes the local project unless you exported a Studio backup first.

Payments and licenses

If you buy Studio, checkout is hosted by Sociobot with Dodo as merchant of record. Line Take Match sends the license token you provide to the Sociobot verification endpoint. Payment details are handled by the merchant and never reach this app. The license and a once-daily verification result are stored in your browser’s local storage.

Network and analytics

The app makes no analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, transcription, or voice-generation requests. Your hosting provider may retain ordinary short-lived access logs for security and reliability.

Your choices

Export a CSV at any time. Studio users can export an audio-inclusive JSON backup. You can remove individual takes, clear site storage, or remove the local license from the unlock panel.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site operator through Sociobot.
